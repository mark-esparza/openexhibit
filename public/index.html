Welcome to OpenExhibit

OpenExhibit finds freely licensed medical images, diagrams and videos on Wikimedia Commons and gives you a ready credit line for each one. It understands medical shorthand, so a search for KF ring finds Kayser Fleischer rings and MI means myocardial infarction.

Search Tips

TryWhat happens
AbbreviationsMI, DKA, BPPV and KF ring are matched to the full medical term before searching.
DiagramsChoose Diagrams for labeled drawings such as the brachial plexus or the cardiac cycle.
DrugsA drug name shows its chemical structure first when one is on record.
VideosChoose Videos for exam signs and maneuvers. Many clips come from open access journal articles.
Wrong meaning?Use the suggestions under Search Details to pick the concept you meant.

About

Every search runs three ways at once: the words you typed, the standard medical name, and the matching Commons category. The meaning of your words comes from Wikidata, ranked by medical identifiers such as MeSH, ICD and anatomy codes. Files found by more than one route rank higher.

OpenExhibit is for education. It is not a diagnostic tool, and images are not reviewed for accuracy. Check each file's description and source before teaching from it.

Licensing

Media belongs to its authors on Wikimedia Commons and keeps its own license, shown with every result. Most licenses require credit. Files marked BY SA also require that anything you build from them uses the same license.
